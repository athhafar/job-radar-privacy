Mobile Job Radar – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 October 2026

Mobile Job Radar is a personal, non-commercial tool built and used by a single developer. It searches publicly available Threads posts for mobile-developer job opportunities and sends a private notification to the owner of the app. It is not offered to the public and has no other users.

What data the app processes

Using the official Threads API (threads_basic and threads_keyword_search), the app reads only public posts that match job-related keywords. For each post it processes:

  • the post text,
  • the public username of the author,
  • the post ID, timestamp and permalink.

The app does not read private accounts, direct messages, follower lists or any non-public information.

How the data is used

  • Posts are filtered by keywords locally on the owner's computer.
  • Matching post text may be sent to the Google Gemini API to judge whether it is a relevant job opportunity and to draft a reply for the owner.
  • A short notification with a link to the original post is sent to the owner's private Telegram chat.

What is stored

The app stores only a local list of post IDs that it has already seen, so that the same post is not reported twice. Post text is not kept in a database and is not republished, sold, shared with third parties or used for advertising, profiling or training models.

Third-party services

  • Meta Threads API – source of public posts. Meta's privacy policy applies.
  • Google Gemini API – evaluates matching post text. Google's privacy policy applies.
  • Telegram – delivers notifications to the owner. Telegram's privacy policy applies.

Data deletion requests

If you authored a Threads post that was processed by this app and want any related data removed, email EMAIL_ANDA@example.com with a link to your post. The post ID will be removed from the local list and any notification containing it will be deleted, within 7 days. Because no other copy of your data is kept, this removes everything the app holds about you.

Users who authorize the app can also revoke access at any time in Threads under Settings → Account → Website permissions.

Changes to this policy

If the app's behavior changes, this page will be updated and the date above revised.
